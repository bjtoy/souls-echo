## 1. Purpose and Use Boundary

This document provides neutral wording for possible later recruitment to five
Early Diagnostic Review problem interviews. It does not authorise recruitment,
screening, participant contact, scheduling, interviews or data collection.

The Early Diagnostic Review tests the interview method, investigates early
negative evidence and identifies whether wider Gate 1 research should continue,
change, pause or receive a stop/pivot review. Five interviews cannot validate the
problem, product, market or Gate 1 and cannot justify Gate 2 progression.

The copy deliberately does not describe the proposed Soul's Echo Band, remote
physical interaction, haptics, light, local inhibit or implementation. Product
concept exposure belongs to a later, separately approved research stage.

Before any copy is used, approve and insert:

- a dedicated project contact method;
- proposed session duration;
- incentive terms or an explicit no-incentive statement;
- recruitment closing date if required;
- document version and effective date;
- approved data locations and retention rules;
- the final recruitment channel and presentation context; and
- all associated governance and interview materials.

Do not select a recruitment platform through this document.

## 2. Required Recruitment Facts

Every operational variant must make clear that:

- Soul's Echo is conducting early product and customer discovery;
- the topic is connection and communication during physical separation;
- initial participants must be Australian adults;
- participation is voluntary;
- the session concerns experiences and existing behaviour;
- no product testing is involved;
- no product has been built;
- selection is not guaranteed;
- participation does not guarantee a future product;
- negative opinions are welcome;
- questions may be skipped and participation may stop at any time;
- sensitive personal disclosure is not required;
- the activity is not counselling, medical, legal, relationship or crisis support;
- recruitment does not add a person to a marketing list; and
- the materials remain subject to operational approval.

## 3. Prohibited Recruitment Framing

Do not advertise the Band or intended solution in the headline or opening pitch.
Do not use claims or framing such as:

- revolutionary;
- magical;
- intimate;
- reconnect with your loved ones;
- transform your relationships;
- never feel apart;
- feel their touch from anywhere;
- validated;
- safe or secure;
- privacy compliant;
- accessible or accessibility compliant;
- university approved;
- commercially available;
- technically feasible; or
- regulatorily compliant.

Do not imply that participants should experience a problem or that a favourable
response is expected.

## 5. Longer Community-Group Post

### Proposed Headline

**Invitation to express interest in early research about staying connected across distance**

### Proposed Copy

Soul's Echo is preparing an early product and customer discovery study about how
Australian adults communicate with people who matter to them when they are
physically apart.

The first stage is intended to examine whether the interview questions work,
whether recent real examples can be discussed and whether the broader research
should continue unchanged, be redesigned, paused or reconsidered. It is not a
product trial. No product has been built, and participants will not be asked to
evaluate a proposed product during these first interviews.

If operational approval is later granted, selected participants would be invited
to a one-to-one discussion about recent experiences, communication methods and
wearable behaviour. It is legitimate to report that existing communication is
fully adequate or that no unmet need exists.

Participation would be voluntary. A participant could decline any question, ask
to change topic, take a break or stop without giving a reason. Negative feedback
is explicitly welcome. Participants should not disclose sensitive personal
information or relationship experiences they are uncomfortable sharing.

This is early product and customer discovery. It is not counselling, medical
advice, legal advice, relationship advice, crisis support or university-approved
research.

Expressing interest:

- does not guarantee selection;
- does not guarantee a future product or product access;
- does not add a person to a marketing list; and
- does not authorise unrelated follow-up.

Initial participants must be Australian adults. Selection would seek variation
in communication context, purchaser or recipient perspective, wearable use or
scepticism, broad location and recruitment source without requiring traumatic
disclosure or another person's identity.
