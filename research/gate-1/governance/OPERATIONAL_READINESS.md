## 1. Purpose and Authority Boundary

This document records the minimum operational decisions for the five-interview
Early Diagnostic Review. It does not authorise recruitment, screening,
participant contact, scheduling, interviews or data collection.

No external cloud, scheduling, recording, transcription or research service is
approved. No participant personal information may be committed to Git.

## 2. Dedicated Project Contact

Use one dedicated Soul's Echo project-contact channel for participant questions,
scheduling, privacy requests and withdrawal requests. The Project Lead monitors
the channel and is responsible for responding appropriately and without
unnecessary delay. Contact information must not be reused for marketing or an
unrelated purpose.

**Approved contact:** [PROJECT LEAD TO SUPPLY]

**External provider assessment:** [REQUIRED BEFORE USE IF A PROVIDER RECEIVES PARTICIPANT PERSONAL INFORMATION]

Do not substitute an unapproved personal address or invent an account.

## 3. Local-First Storage and Access

The initial arrangement is local-first storage on one Project Lead-controlled
device. Before collection, the Project Lead must approve the actual device and
location and confirm an access-controlled non-shared account, device encryption,
strong login protection, automatic locking, current operating-system security,
no shared household account, no uncontrolled removable media and no participant
personal information in Git.

Use separate folders or logical records for contact and scheduling information,
the participant-ID mapping, consent records, raw notes, de-identified notes and
analysis. A recordings area is not required because the first five interviews
are notes-only. The participant-ID mapping must be separated from notes and
receive the most restricted access.

Initial access is limited to the Project Lead. Additional access requires an
explicit role-based approval. Cloud or synchronised storage requires privacy and
security approval before participant information is placed there.

**Approved device and location:** [PROJECT LEAD APPROVAL REQUIRED]

**Confirmed controls:** [PROJECT LEAD CONFIRMATION REQUIRED]

## 4. Backup Treatment

One controlled backup may be used only after the Project Lead approves its
location, encryption, access, retention, expiry and deletion behaviour. It may
cover consent records, operational records, notes and analysis. No recording
backup is permitted for the first five interviews because recording is
prohibited.

Backup access is limited initially to the Project Lead. Participant information
must not be placed in a cloud or synchronisation service merely to create a
backup. Participants must not be promised immediate deletion from immutable or
delayed-expiry backups. A deleted active record may persist in an approved backup
until normal expiry and must not be restored to active use.

**Approved backup process or explicit category-level no-backup decision:** [PROJECT LEAD APPROVAL REQUIRED]

## 5. Provisional Gate 1 Retention Rules

These are provisional project rules, not statements of legal minimums. An
assessed legal, university, professional or other obligation may require an
approved amendment before collection.

- **Unselected screener responses:** delete after Early Diagnostic Review
  recruitment, selection and eligibility review are complete; retain only
  de-identified recruitment-source counts needed for bias review.
- **Scheduling and contact details:** delete when the session, applicable
  withdrawal opportunity, incentive administration and specifically consented
  follow-up are complete.
- **Participant-ID mapping:** delete after the Gate 1 decision and applicable
  withdrawal opportunity are complete.
- **Consent records:** retain through Gate 1 closure and applicable governance
  review, then delete unless an assessed obligation requires longer retention.
- **Recordings:** none may be created for the first five interviews.
- **Raw interviewer notes:** delete after expanded notes are checked,
  de-identified and no longer required to honour the applicable withdrawal
  opportunity.
- **Pseudonymised or de-identified notes:** retain through Gate 1 decision-making
  and contradiction review, then review for further de-identification or deletion.
- **Follow-up-contact permission and contact:** retain only while the specifically
  approved purpose remains necessary, or until permission is withdrawn.
- **Aggregated findings:** retain as project evidence while useful, subject to
  review of residual re-identification risk.

The withdrawal milestone cannot become operational until participant document
versions, effective dates and any overriding obligations are approved.

## 6. Notes-Only Operating Procedure

The first five interviews must use notes only. No audio or video recording and no
transcription are permitted.

The interviewer must take contemporaneous notes using the participant identifier;
avoid unnecessary identifying details; distinguish participant evidence from
researcher interpretation; mark uncertain wording as uncertain or paraphrased;
never invent verbatim quotations; expand notes promptly after the session,
preferably the same day; preserve contradictions and alternative explanations;
and promptly review and de-identify the expanded record.

Notes-only participation remains valid within the acknowledged limits of note
accuracy. Recording cannot be offered during this review.

## 7. Interviewer Responsibility

The Project Lead approves and identifies the interviewer. The interviewer must
follow the approved guide, avoid concept contamination, honour consent choices,
avoid unnecessary probing, stop or redirect on distress, protect participant
information, record contradictory evidence and complete de-identification
promptly.

If the Project Lead conducts interviews, founder bias must be mitigated through
the neutral script, structured notes, explicit alternative explanations,
contradiction logging and the per-interview protocol review.

**Assigned interviewer and identifier:** [PROJECT LEAD APPROVAL REQUIRED]

## 8. Session Duration

The target duration is **35 minutes**. The maximum planned duration is **45
minutes**. A participant may stop earlier, skip any question or decline to cover
every topic. The interviewer must not pressure a participant to use the full time
or complete the guide.

## 9. Recruitment Channels and Sample-Control Rule

Permitted channel categories for later approval are public recruitment, suitable
general community groups, limited referrals and limited founder-network
invitations, with broad Australian reach where practical.

No more than **two of the five** participants may be founder-connected.
Founder-connected participants must not dominate a perspective represented in
the group. Record only the broad recruitment-source category required for bias
review.

Exact channels remain subject to Project Lead approval. Do not approach
workplaces, Defence organisations, universities, health services,
domestic-violence services or another organisation involving sensitivity or a
power relationship without separate approval.

**Exact approved channels:** [PROJECT LEAD APPROVAL REQUIRED]

## 10. Scheduling

Schedule manually through the approved dedicated project contact. Collect one
scheduling contact method after selection where practical. Do not access or
collect calendars, contact lists or unnecessary information. No scheduling
platform is approved.

## 11. Incentive

A modest incentive is recommended to recognise the 35–45 minute burden, improve
fairness and reduce reliance on founder-connected volunteers. It must be
proportionate, disclosed before consent, independent of favourable feedback or
answering every question, and reasonably earned if a participant stops early.

**Amount and payment process:** [PROJECT LEAD APPROVAL REQUIRED]

No incentive may be promised until those terms and any service receiving payment
information are separately approved.

## 12. Versions and Effective Dates

Every operational participant-facing document must state its document version,
approval date and effective date. A participant must receive the version current
for their session, and the consent record must identify every version used.
Superseded copies remain in repository history but must not be issued.

Versions, approval dates and effective dates remain Project Lead decisions.
