## 1. Introduction

Soul's Echo is planning early product and customer discovery about communication,
physical separation, wearables, privacy and personal control. The proposed Band
is only a concept.

Completing this screener is voluntary, does not guarantee selection, does not add
someone to marketing and should not require traumatic disclosure or another
person's identity.

This screener does not itself authorise collection. It cannot be used until real
contact details, provisional retention rules, data locations, document versions
and effective dates are approved and every participant-facing placeholder is
removed.

## 2. Early Diagnostic Review Reduced Screener

The first five problem interviews are an **Early Diagnostic Review**, not product
or market validation. Use only this reduced field set for those interviews:

1. adult eligibility;
2. voluntary participation;
3. broad separation or relationship context;
4. likely recipient or purchaser perspective;
5. wearable use or scepticism;
6. broad Australian location;
7. recruitment source; and
8. one approved scheduling contact collected only after selection.

Do not collect optional accessibility details, recording preference, detailed
maintenance tolerance or other non-essential screening information during the
Early Diagnostic Review unless a later approved reason requires it. Recording
consent belongs at the interview-consent stage.

Positive findings from five participants cannot validate the problem, market,
product or Gate 1. Strong negative evidence may justify pausing and reviewing the
method.

## 4. Recording

Do not request recording preference for initial selection during the Early
Diagnostic Review. Recording consent is a separate affirmative decision at the
interview-consent stage after full information is provided.

For a later wider stage, any question about willingness to consider recording is
planning information only and never consent. Declining recording must not
automatically exclude someone where notes are reasonably sufficient.

## 5. Exclusion and Escalation Rules

Exclude from the initial approved method:

- minors;
- people unable to provide informed consent;
- people whose participation appears required or pressured;
- people seeking emergency, legal, medical, counselling or crisis assistance from the project; and
- situations where the approved method would create inappropriate risk.

Escalate for governance review where voluntariness or comprehension is unclear,
an organisation has a power relationship, a materially different method is
requested, sensitive-topic handling appears inadequate, university requirements
may apply or the researcher is uncertain whether proceeding is appropriate.

Do not automatically exclude someone because they have experienced a difficult
relationship. Do not intentionally recruit survivors of abuse on that basis or
probe unnecessarily. Dangerous-scenario research requires an appropriately
reviewed method and may require specialist expertise.

## 6. Researcher-Only Selection Record

Use a sequential identifier such as `P001`. Do not encode relationship type,
gender, age, location, accessibility information or outcome.

Sample-balance fields may be recorded separately only when approved and
necessary. Record selection outcome as potentially eligible, not selected for
sample balance, ineligible, requires clarification or research not appropriate
for the assistance sought.

Do not record speculative diagnoses or unnecessary safety detail.

## 7. Incentive Principles

No amount or payment platform is approved. Any later incentive must be modest,
proportionate to time and burden, disclosed before consent and independent of
positive feedback or answering every question. Do not withhold reasonably earned
payment because a participant stops.

## 8. Data Handling and Retention

Before this screener is used, approve data locations, access roles, backup
treatment and provisional rules for unselected responses, selected responses,
contact details and recruitment-source data. These rules must not be described as
legally required unless established.

Contact data must be separated from research notes and must not be reused for
unrelated marketing. Participant personal information must not be committed to
Git. External screening or recruitment tools require privacy and security review
before receiving any response.

## 9. University Status

This is product and customer discovery and is not represented as
university-approved research. Applicable university requirements must be checked
before any activity connected to formal academic research, publication,
university resources, university recruitment or assessment involving human
participants.
